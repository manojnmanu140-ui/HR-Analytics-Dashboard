**HR Analytics Dashboard | Power BI**

Project Overview
An interactive HR Analytics Dashboard built using Microsoft Power BI to analyze employee attrition, workforce demographics, job satisfaction, and salary patterns.

## Objectives
- Analyze employee attrition across departments and genders.
- Understand attrition patterns across age and monthly income groups.
- Examine job satisfaction across departments.
- Track key workforce metrics through interactive KPI cards and slicers.

## Tools & Technologies
- Microsoft Power BI
- DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
- Data Visualization
- HR Analytics

## Dashboard Features
- **Total Employees:** 1,470
- **Employees Who Left:** 237
- **Active Employees:** 1,233
- **Attrition Rate:** 16.12%
- **Average Attrition Age:** 36.92

### Visualizations
- Attrition by Department
- Job Satisfaction by Rating
- Attrition by Gender
- Attrition by Age Group
- Attrition by Education Field
- Attrition by Salary Range

## Key Findings
- 237 out of 1,470 employees left, giving an overall attrition rate of approximately 16.12%.
- The dashboard compares attrition across departments, gender, age groups, education fields, and salary ranges.
- Interactive Job Level filtering allows users to explore workforce metrics by seniority.
